It's like we always chase vintage aesthetics to feel something. We're constantly jumping in the past to feel the simpler times but for once I feel like appreciating the present. There's something so so soooo cool about living and existing with the current technology that allows me to spontaneously make websites and they probably look shitty and are super unsafe but it's what i have today.

I used to be obsessed with technology as a kid; religiously used to watch unboxing, and reveal videos for new phones and computers constantly. I feel like that era gets sidelined from pop culture and the way it shaped our generation and our aesthetics. I will never forget the iphone 5, 5s or 5c - 5c I actually had, the light blue one and i thought i was the coolest fucking kid in the school - the first day i brought it to school i didn't tell anyone i had it, just put it on my desk face down, letting people come up to me instead - super nonchalant from day one.
